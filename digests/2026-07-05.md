## California (CA)

- **UPDATED** [AB1856](https://legiscan.com/CA/bill/AB1856/2025) — Age verification signals: software applications.
  Last action (2026-07-01): Read second time and amended. Re-referred to Com. on APPR.
  _Requires software applications to detect and honor age verification signals — a direct technical implementation mandate._

- **UPDATED** [AB1709](https://legiscan.com/CA/bill/AB1709/2025) — Covered platforms: age restriction: e-Safety Advisory Commission.
  Last action (2026-06-23): From committee: Do pass and re-refer to Com. on JUD. (Ayes 7. Noes 0.) (June 22). Re-referred to Com. on JUD.
  _Bans under-16 social media accounts and requires covered platforms to verify user identity/age before access — a technical age-verification build._

- **UPDATED** [AB2246](https://legiscan.com/CA/bill/AB2246/2025) — Online service, product, or feature: access by children.
  Last action (2026-07-01): From committee: Do pass and re-refer to Com. on APPR. (Ayes 13. Noes 0.) (June 30). Re-referred to Com. on APPR.
  _Youth Social Media Protection Act requires default privacy settings, disabling default geolocation/profiling for minors, and age estimation — specific technical builds._

  Last action (2026-07-02): Read second time. Ordered to third reading.
